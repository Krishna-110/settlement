Delete Your Settlement Account

Settlement (com.cleardues) — last updated 1 September 2026

This page explains how to delete your Settlement account and what happens to your data. It applies to the Settlement app published on Google Play.

How to request deletion

  1. Email gaurav.tekkie@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Settlement account.
  2. Use the subject line Delete my account.
  3. Include the phone number registered in the app, so we can locate your record.

We will confirm by email once the deletion is complete. Requests are actioned within 30 days.

We only accept requests sent from the account's own email address. This is to make sure nobody else can delete your data.

Deactivating instead of deleting

If you want to stop using Settlement but keep the option of returning, open Account → Deactivate account in the app. Deactivating hides your profile from other users and stops SMS notifications, but keeps your data. Deactivation is reversible; deletion is not.

What is kept, and why

An expense in Settlement is shared between two people. If you recorded an expense with someone else, that record is part of their ledger as well as yours. Deleting your account does not remove it from their account, because doing so would silently alter another person's financial records without their consent.

Those records remain visible to the other party. They are not used for any other purpose, and your contact details are removed from them.

Retention

Deleted data is removed from our active database within 30 days of the request. Residual copies in encrypted backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle and are gone within 90 days. We do not retain deleted account data for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.
