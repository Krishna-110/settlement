Privacy Policy

Settlement (com.cleardues) — last updated 1 September 2026

Settlement is a record-keeping app for tracking shared expenses between people you know. It does not move money, connect to your bank, or process payments. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

1. Data we collect

DataWhySource
Name, email address, profile picture To create and identify your account Your Google account, when you sign in
Phone number So friends can find you, and to send debt notifications by SMS Entered by you in the app
Contact names and phone numbers To show which of your contacts already use Settlement Your device, only after you grant contacts permission
Expense records — amounts, notes, who owes whom, dates The core function of the app Entered by you and by people you share expenses with
Group names and membership To organise expenses by trip, flat, or team Entered by you

2. Contacts permission

Granting contacts access is optional; the rest of the app works without it. When you grant it, phone numbers from your contacts are sent to our server to check which of them match existing Settlement accounts. Numbers that do not match an account are used only for that check and are not retained. Numbers that do match are stored so we can show that person as a friend you can add.

You can revoke contacts permission at any time in your device's Android settings.

3. Who we share data with

  • Google — used for sign-in. Google provides us your name, email, and profile picture. We do not send Google any of your expense data.
  • Vonage — our SMS provider. When someone records an expense involving you, we send Vonage your phone number and the notification text, which includes the other person's name and the amount, so the message can be delivered.

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. Settlement contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in a PostgreSQL database on our server. All traffic between the app and the server uses HTTPS. Your login session is held on your device in Android's encrypted SecureStore, not in plain storage.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can deactivate your account from Settings inside the app, which hides your profile from other users.

To request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data, email gaurav.tekkie@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We will action the request within 30 days.

Note that expense records are shared by nature: an expense you recorded with another person remains visible to that person as part of their own ledger.

6. Children

Settlement is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify users in the app.
